Player process layer preview

Every estimate is a cutoff-safe predictive rate per 90 minutes for a player's own subsequent attacking process, conditioned on exposure. Shooting and creation are separate components and are never summed. These are conditional predictive estimates, not causal player value.

Role-relative is the estimate divided by the role population rate at the cutoff, so 2.00 means twice an ordinary player in that role. The band shows ±1 total log standard deviation. Click a row to open the player.

Where the estimate comes from

Uncertainty

Mapping uncertainty is shared by every player and largely cancels in a comparison. Player-local uncertainty comes from this player's own exposure and does not cancel.

Evidence by club spell

The ratio compares predicted rates directly. Overlapping single-player bands are not a comparison; the interval here is on the difference, which is what decides whether two players are actually separated.

Each estimate is frozen on the day of the player's first appearance for the new club, so no evidence from that club reaches it. A club change here is a retrospective label, not a claim that a transfer was known in advance.

CRPS is a proper score; lower is better. The paired interval resamples by player, so an ever-present player counts once rather than as many independent cases. A starred interval excludes zero.
