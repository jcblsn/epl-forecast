# Research judgment

Separate three questions when evaluating a new information layer:

1. Is the information source or latent concept plausibly useful?
2. Does this implementation represent it well, with identifiable parameters?
3. Is there sufficient empirical evidence to use it operationally?

Proper chronological scores and genuinely forward forecasts dominate the third
question. A failed implementation does not automatically answer the first two.
xG, squad quality, availability and player composition have strong football
motivations. Normally give such a channel a bounded second, materially different,
principled formulation, especially when oracle, high-information or directional
diagnostics suggest useful information is present. State the hypothesis, comparison,
information cutoff and stopping condition before running it.

Include oracle or high-information conditions where feasible. Oracle gains without
deployable gains point to information availability; failure in both calls for an
identification and representation check. Retain important slice, calibration,
state-interpretability or complementary-error signals long enough to test the joint
model, while withholding operational promotion. After multiple distinct credible
formulations fail even with high-information inputs and yield no such signal,
park the idea. Prior belief does not justify endless iteration.

Prefer coherent generative structure, identifiable parameters, meaningful
uncertainty and reusable football concepts over small gains from special cases.
Boundary heuristics may enable a prototype; do not turn them into architecture by
tuning historical match loss. The current squad carry-forward probability, player
prior scale, lineup pseudo-counts, substitution probabilities and linear injury
recovery remain explicit assumptions until learned or better-observed replacements
are justified.

M2 remains the unchanged operational benchmark. M5/M6 supply the research lineage:
dynamic Quality/Tilt, propagated uncertainty, evolving season paths and player
information. M7 first adds probabilistic team xG observations and removes common
Tilt/scoring redundancy; player reformulation follows that stronger parent.
Preserve prospective M5/M6 evidence throughout. Keep each batch's evidence compact:
one report, machine-readable summaries and reproducible or prospective inputs.
