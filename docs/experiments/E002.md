# E002: Elo with an ordered-logit outcome layer

## Protocol frozen before the first E002 evaluation

Question: does a simple result-based rating process improve H/D/A forecasts over
M2, the attack/defense Poisson reference? This is a distinct benchmark using the
same scores, teams and daily information cutoff. No new data source or credentials
are needed. The E001 implementation and evidence are preserved at Git commit
`6277385`.

The approach is informed by Hvattum and Arntzen's use of Elo-derived covariates in
ordered-logit match prediction. Their publisher abstract and preview were
accessible through search; the full publisher page returned HTTP 403. This is our
own fully specified baseline, not a reproduction of their fitted model or a claim
to implement ClubElo. [Hvattum and Arntzen (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2009.10.002)

## Fixed specification

The configuration is [elo.toml](../../configs/elo.toml). M0 and M2 retain their
E001 settings. Every forecast date uses the same preceding 1,095 calendar days of
Premier League results, with at least 700 training matches. All ratings start at
zero at the beginning of each training-window replay; an unseen club also starts
at zero. There is no seasonal reset, promotion offset, goal-margin multiplier or
cross-division input. The zero prior represents the initial rating population;
it need not equal the current participating clubs' average.

For a home/away rating difference `d`, the Elo expected result is
`s = logistic(log(10) * (d + 60) / 400)`. The observed result is 1, 0.5 or 0 for
a home win, draw or away win. After the date's matches, home ratings receive
`20 * (result - s)` and away ratings receive the opposite. Every match on a date
uses ratings from before that date; sorted match IDs make the replay deterministic.
The constants 20 and 60 are initial engineering choices, not optimized estimates.

An ordered-logit layer maps `x = d / 400` to three outcome probabilities. With
`eta = intercept + slope * x` and a positive draw threshold `t`:

```text
P(H) = logistic(eta - t)
P(A) = logistic(-eta - t)
P(D) = logistic(t - eta) - logistic(-t - eta)
```

Fit the intercept, nonnegative slope and positive threshold on pre-update rating
differences recorded while replaying the training window. Historical covariates
must never be recomputed from final fitted ratings. The likelihood includes a
ridge penalty of `0.5 * sum((parameter - prior)^2)` with prior
`(log(10) * 60 / 400, log(10), log(5/3))`. This gives a 25% draw probability at
zero linear predictor and keeps sparse training cases finite. The numerical
threshold lower bound is `1e-6`. Calibration rows have equal weights and include
the replay's initial rating warm-up; that initialization is a model limitation.

This model returns outcome probabilities only. It cannot supply goal difference
or score likelihood and is therefore not a replacement for the season simulator's
score model.

## Evaluation and decision rule

Use the E001 chronological periods unchanged: 2015/16–2022/23 development,
2023/24–2024/25 validation, and 2025/26 later-period diagnostics. Every period has
already been inspected during E001. Label E002 retrospective in its configuration,
run metadata and generated report; `--split holdout` selects the historical
interval and does not restore its independence.

There is one candidate and no hyperparameter sweep or retuning after scores are
seen. Report log loss, Brier, classwise calibration, season-level scores, matched
market comparisons and the existing paired 28-day block-bootstrap intervals.
Markets retain their collection-time caveat. Negative candidate-minus-reference
log-loss differences favor the candidate.

To provisionally prefer M3 for outcome forecasts, require a development log-loss
gain and a validation gain of at least 0.005, with the validation paired 95%
interval entirely below zero and no validation Brier regression. Otherwise keep
M2 as the reference and retain M3 as a comparison model. The 2025/26 results are
secondary diagnostics. Passing this rule would justify a historical preference,
not establish performance on an untouched future test set.

Verify the rating update, same-day batch invariance, chronological calibration
covariates, analytic likelihood gradient, probabilities at extreme rating gaps,
unseen clubs and invariance to same-day/future target mutations. Compare the
unchanged M0/M2 forecasts with E001 and repeat E002 from the pinned data to check
deterministic results.

## Results

Implementation and evaluation completed on 2026-09-05. The protocol was committed
as `5452df5` and the tested model as `1be7f91`, before inspecting its evaluation
results. Each model made 4,180 chronological predictions across the three periods.

| Period | Matches | M2 log loss | M3 log loss | M2 Brier | M3 Brier | M2 ECE | M3 ECE |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development | 3,040 | 0.97176 | 0.97758 | 0.57688 | 0.58009 | 0.01462 | 0.02474 |
| Validation | 760 | 0.95734 | 0.96422 | 0.56782 | 0.57298 | 0.01870 | 0.02608 |
| Later 2025/26, retrospective | 380 | 1.02649 | 1.03105 | 0.61569 | 0.62053 | 0.04505 | 0.05619 |

The paired M3-minus-M2 log-loss differences are:

| Period | Difference | Paired 95% interval | Blocks |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development | +0.00582 | [−0.00009, +0.01205] | 86 |
| Validation | +0.00687 | [−0.00199, +0.01551] | 22 |
| Later 2025/26 | +0.00456 | [−0.01378, +0.02151] | 11 |

Keep M2 as the provisional reference and M3 as a comparison model. M3 fails all
four prespecified gates: development log loss, validation minimum gain, validation
interval and validation Brier. Its log loss is higher in both validation seasons;
it improves on M2 in two of eight development seasons. All three paired intervals
include zero, so these results do not establish that Elo is generally inferior.
They provide no evidence to replace M2 with this particular specification. No
settings were changed after these results were seen.

M3 improves substantially on the league-frequency control, whose log losses are
1.06530, 1.06870 and 1.08371. The average-closing market's matched validation log
loss is 0.93372 and its later-period value is 1.01177; market collection times
remain unavailable and closing quotes have a later information horizon. Full
per-season results, calibration bins and matched comparisons are retained under
[E002](E002/), together with the [machine-readable decision](E002/decision.json).

## Diagnostics and limits

Adding a fitted draw mechanism does not itself fix draw calibration. Mean draw
probabilities are below the observed frequencies for M3 in all three periods:

| Period | Observed draws | M2 mean P(draw) | M3 mean P(draw) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Development | 23.39% | 23.60% | 22.02% |
| Validation | 23.03% | 22.29% | 20.87% |
| Later 2025/26 | 27.37% | 23.55% | 22.41% |

The draw layer was fitted to historical pre-update features, including the replay's
initial warm-up, and then applied to final ratings at each cutoff. This can produce
a different distribution of rating gaps; a burn-in or chronological calibration
ablation would be a separate experiment. Other untested choices include K, home
advantage, seasonal carryover and the promotion prior. This experiment cannot
identify which choice accounts for its scores. It also supplies no goal counts,
so M2 remains the score-generating season model regardless of the outcome gate.

All periods were already available to the research process. The retrospective
labels in `summary.json`, `run.json` and the generated report must remain visible.
A future model-selection claim needs a new outer evaluation with all tuning
restricted to earlier dates.

```sh
uv sync --locked
uv run python scripts/reproduce.py --config configs/elo.toml --output runs/E002-repeat

  --model M3-elo-ordered-logit-v1 --season 2024-2025 --date 2024-08-17 \
  --home arsenal --away wolverhampton-wanderers --output runs/elo-match.json
```

The reproduction restores the 32 pinned cached source files, independently
normalizes them twice, repeats the 380-match archive cross-check and all three
evaluation periods, and runs the reference M2 season simulation. Its simulation
and example forecast use M2; the separate command above exercises M3. No new live
download or credentials were needed for this repeat.

All prediction files, market predictions, summaries and run metadata were
byte-identical between the first E002 run and its reproduction. The 8,360 repeated
M0/M2 forecast rows match E001 exactly, and the 10,000-draw M2 simulation has the
same checksum as E001. The standalone M3 forecast matches its rolling-evaluation
row. The original E001 code fingerprint matches the source at commit `6277385`.
See [verification.json](E002/verification.json) for checksums and commands.

The expanded test suite has 54 passing tests, including the Elo checks specified
above. Ruff, the end-to-end reproduction and an isolated installation of the

environment retained the earlier wheel at the same version. The next comparison is a valid
