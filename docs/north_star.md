# North star

Build a continuously updated probabilistic forecasting product for the Premier
League and Championship. Forecasts and clear explanations of their material
changes are the product.

From a coherent view of the two leagues, provide:

- Score distributions and home/draw/away probabilities for every fixture.
- Distributions for final points and every finishing position.
- Title, European qualification, relegation, automatic promotion, playoff and
  promotion probabilities under the applicable competition rules.
- Uncertainty-aware responses to injuries, transfers and lineup changes.
- Continuity for clubs moving between the Premier League and Championship.
- Separately measurable structural and market-assisted forecasts.
- An archived forecast record that makes accuracy and material changes inspectable.

Make uncertainty, information timing and conditional scenario assumptions clear.
Historical evaluation and forecasts archived before kickoff should show whether
the product improves. External forecasting systems are sources of useful ideas,
not specifications to reproduce.
